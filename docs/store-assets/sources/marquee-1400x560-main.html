The quiet return of long-form reading.

By E. Tanaka — Mar 2026

In a web designed to interrupt, the simplest act of finishing an article has become a small, deliberate decision — one made against a tide of sidebars, autoplay, and scrolling promotions.

The writing itself hasn't changed. What changed is the room it arrives in. Strip away the room, and the sentence is allowed to do its work again.
