Josh Shapiro warns Democrats face battle over direction

Shapiro distanced himself from Darializa Avila Chevalier

By CJ Womack · Fox News
Published July 5, 2026 6:00pm EDT

In the wake of democratic socialist Darializa Avila Chevalier defeating New York Rep. Adriano Espaillat in the 13th Congressional District primary, Josh Shapiro is calling on Democrats to reckon with the direction of the party and what it believes going forward, warning of a battle ahead.

Shapiro warned that the party faces a battle over what it believes, urging colleagues to focus on delivering tangible results rather than litigating ideological purity tests that alienate the broader electorate.
