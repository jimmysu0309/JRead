Why steering wheels should always be round, not square

I have an affliction that, when I begin to think about how difficult it is to find a pleasing steering wheel in a new car, begins to play on repeat in my head.

Audi had more than 100 steering wheel designs – so over time it will have one too, of course

I've learned to live with it as something as inevitable as getting into a new car and being faced with an unnaturally awkward primary control surface.

The square steering wheel was so widely ridiculed when it featured in the 1973 Austin Allegro that the industry gave up on them for a while.

I still can't pinpoint the exact moment that un-round steering wheels became the term even though I suspect it isn't grammatically accurate.
