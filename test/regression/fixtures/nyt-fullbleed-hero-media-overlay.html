Too Many Books?

When a landlord objected to the 10,000 books in a New York apartment, a crisis began for its tenant.

Mendel Uminer does not think of himself as a hoarder, but the management of his building on the Upper East Side came to a different conclusion after inspecting the roughly ten thousand volumes stacked in every corner of his studio apartment over the years.

The collection began in graduate school and grew steadily through estate sales, closing bookstores and the generosity of retiring scholars, until the shelves gave out and the floor took over.

Friends helped him pack the collection into boxes when the moving date finally arrived, a process that took the better part of a week and a great deal of tape.
