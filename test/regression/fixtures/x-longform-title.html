How to remember everything you read (stop trying)
If you need to remember it, it is not important. If it is important, you will remember it. For some reason this is the part that everyone gets backwards when they start building a system.

How to actually learn anything fast

The bottleneck was never storage. It was retrieval, and retrieval only strengthens when you force yourself to reconstruct the idea without looking at your notes.

The Second Subconscious (Delete Your Note-Taking System)

Most note-taking systems are elaborate procrastination. They feel like progress because they produce artifacts, but the artifacts are not the point.
