How to Tie a Tie

  1. Drape the tie around your neck so the wide end hangs roughly twelve inches lower than the narrow end, giving you enough length to wrap and tuck the knot without running short before you finish tying it.

  2. Cross the wide end over the narrow end near your collar, keeping the crossing point snug against your neck so the finished knot sits cleanly rather than drooping loosely down the front of your shirt all day.

Community Q&A

Question

Can I use a scarf as a tie instead of a normal one?

Community Answer

Yes, you can use a scarf as a tie if it is long enough.

Question

What type of knot should I use for a school uniform?

Community Answer

The four-in-hand knot should work fine for most school looks.

Question

What is the quickest way to tie a tie in a hurry?

Community Answer

The four-in-hand knot is quickest. It barely takes a minute.

Question

Does the knot type depend on my collar style at all?

Community Answer

Yes, wider spread collars usually pair better with a Windsor.
