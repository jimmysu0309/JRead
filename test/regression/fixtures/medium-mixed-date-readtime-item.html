The slowest disk drive ever sold

And how people made it the fastest

Enzo Lombardi
15 min read·5 days ago

The Commodore 1541 is widely called the slowest consumer disk drive ever made. Out of the box, paired with a Commodore 64, it transferred data at roughly 400 bytes per second and everyone simply accepted it.

Thirty years later, software running on the exact same unmodified hardware loads the same data at about sixteen thousand bytes per second, a forty-fold improvement extracted by hobbyists.
