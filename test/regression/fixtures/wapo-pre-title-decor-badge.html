Why Europe won’t have the new Siri

The Digital Markets Act was supposed to create more choices. Instead, people get worse technology.

In the name of fairness and competition, Europe has written laws that leave its citizens as the only people in the free world without access to the most advanced technology available today.

Case in point: Apple’s newly announced artificial intelligence assistant will launch on iPhones this fall in London and Toronto, but not in Paris or Berlin under heavy-handed European tech regulation.
