What drives health spending in the U.S. compared to other countries

Health spending per capita in the United States continues to outpace comparable countries by a wide margin. In 2021, the United States spent over eight thousand dollars per capita on health, while comparable countries averaged less than five thousand dollars per capita on similar baskets of services and goods.

For example, increased spending on administration was the cause of seven percent of spending growth between 2013 and 2021 in the U.S., but only caused three percent of spending growth in comparable countries during the same observation window, a gap that compounds over time.
