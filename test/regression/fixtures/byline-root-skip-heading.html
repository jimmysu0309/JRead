WarTalk: The BS Détente

Ely Ratner on Taiwan and allies

Jordan Schneider
Jun 25, 2026

Ely Ratner, former Assistant Secretary of Defense for Indo-Pacific Security Affairs and now a principal at the Marathon Initiative, joins WarTalk to discuss the détente.

We get into why the so-called détente with Beijing is leaving allies unwilling to stick their necks out, and whether supporting Taiwan is a better dollar-for-dollar return.
