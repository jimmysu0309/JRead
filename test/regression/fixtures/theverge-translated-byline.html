If you asked me a week ago to recommend a thinner PopSockets magnetic phone grip alternative, I would have told you to buy the OhSnap Snap Grip instead because it stayed reliably attached.

The new Low-Pro grip from PopSockets is about as thick as two dimes stacked together when collapsed, making it one of the thinnest MagSafe-compatible grips you can currently buy for a modern smartphone.
