Google's I/O 2026 keynote today was once again full of AI related announcements including a new family of Gemini 3.5 AI models, new features for Search and Gmail, and updates about its Project Aura smart glasses partnership that aims to bring ambient computing to everyday eyewear.

Google launched updated AI models at I/O starting with Gemini 3.5 Flash, with Gemini 3.5 Pro following next month. Starting today, Gemini 3.5 Flash will be the default model for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search, with the company promising faster responses and better handling of agentic tasks.
