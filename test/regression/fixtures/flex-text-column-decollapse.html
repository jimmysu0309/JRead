David Hockney's California (1965)

In 1964, David Hockney left London for California. Having graduated from the Royal College of Art in 1962, he had saved up enough money from selling his paintings to live in Los Angeles for a year.

It occurred to Hockney that the affluent Los Angeles suburbs had rarely been represented in art. He resolved to become the Piranesi of Santa Monica and Beverly Hills.

Sold for £18,710,000

Not only did Los Angeles offer the artist a fresh new world to depict, it also offered him a more liberated way of living than the London he had left behind that year.
