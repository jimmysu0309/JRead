David Hockney Paintings: A Collector's Guide

MAIN_MARK David Hockney is one of the most influential British artists of the twentieth century, working across painting, drawing, printmaking and photography over a career spanning more than six decades.

MAIN_MARK His double portraits of the 1970s, including the celebrated drawing of Christopher Isherwood and Don Bachardy, distil an entire relationship into a few economical lines and carefully observed gestures.

MAIN_MARK The pared-back line drawings reward slow looking: every contour is the product of intense observation, and nothing is wasted in the composition or the surrounding negative space of the sheet.

MAIN_MARK Across the decade Hockney returned again and again to the people closest to him, building a body of work that doubles as an intimate record of his social and creative circle in California and London.
