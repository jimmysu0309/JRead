Why Europe won’t have the new Siri

In the name of fairness and competition, Europe has written laws that leave its citizens as the only people in the free world without access to the most advanced technology available now.

Case in point: Apple’s newly announced AI assistant will launch on iPhones this fall in London and Toronto, but not in Paris or Berlin under heavy-handed European tech regulation today.

Brussels insists the decision rests with Apple, but the company says the rules make it impossible to ship the feature on the timeline that European users would otherwise have enjoyed.
