Complaint Accuses Attorney of Crypto Conflict in His Role at DOJ

An ethics watchdog group filed a complaint Thursday seeking an investigation into whether a top official at the Justice Department broke federal conflict-of-interest law when he issued a new prosecution policy that benefits the cryptocurrency industry while owning digital assets himself.

The complaint comes after an investigation revealed last month that the official owned a substantial amount of crypto-related assets when he ordered an end to investigations into crypto exchanges, raising questions about whether ethics rules were properly followed at the department.

Ethics experts said the case highlights gaps in financial disclosure requirements for senior officials, particularly when emerging asset classes are involved and the rules have not caught up with the technology being regulated by those same officials.
