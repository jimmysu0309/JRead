Google's I/O 2026 keynote today was once again full of AI-related announcements including a new family of Gemini 3.5 AI models, new features for Search and Gmail, and updates about its Project Aura smart glasses. If you weren't able to tune into the livestream today, you can catch up on everything you missed in our roundup below. Google launched updated AI models at I/O, starting with Gemini 3.5 Flash, with Gemini 3.5 Pro following next month. Starting today, Gemini 3.5 Flash will be the default model for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. Google says the new model is significantly faster, better at handling agentic tasks, and more capable across coding, reasoning, and long-context work than the generation it replaces, and it is rolling the update out to developers through the Gemini API and to enterprise customers through Vertex AI over the coming weeks as part of a broader push.
