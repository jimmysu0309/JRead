Microsoft lets users exile floating Copilot button

Microsoft has made Copilot a little less in-your-face with the option to banish the assistant's floating action button to the toolbar. The change, rolling out this week, comes after howls of outrage from customers over the decision to drop a Copilot button onto user workspaces without asking anyone first.

Although the desire to get users clicking on the assistant is understandable, obscuring content in productivity applications was perhaps not the best way to do it. Forums show plenty of frustration with the floating button, and redesigns and interface tweaks will always generate strong feelings from users.
