How Britain Became as Poor as Mississippi

The United Kingdom has spent the better part of two decades sliding down the league tables of rich-world prosperity, a decline so steady and so broad that economists have run out of euphemisms to describe it without sounding alarmist about the trajectory.

To understand how a country with London at its center could come to resemble one of America's poorest states in real terms requires looking past the headline figures and into the machinery of productivity that has quietly seized up.
