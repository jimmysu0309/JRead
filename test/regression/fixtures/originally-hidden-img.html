Health spending growth by category

The chart above displays the change in per capita health spending in each category as a share of the overall increase in spending between 2013 and 2021. Increased spending on administration drove a substantial share of the growth in the United States relative to peer nations.

In 2013, the United States spent over eight thousand dollars per capita on health, while comparable countries averaged less than five thousand dollars. Health spending grew at a similar rate in both the U.S. and comparable countries over this period, but the dollar gap widened.

Notably, growth in spending on long-term care represented a far smaller share of spending growth in the U.S. versus comparable countries, where long-term care investments expanded access to home services for aging populations.
