How LLMs Actually Work

This article walks through how large language models actually work, from the raw text you type all the way to the tokens they generate in response. Modern LLMs are built by stacking Transformer blocks, so once you understand a single block you have understood the whole machine and can reason about its behaviour.

We will cover tokenization, embeddings, the attention mechanism, the feed-forward network, and the residual stream that carries information forward through the layers, building a complete mental model of the pipeline from input to output one stage at a time.

By the end you should have a solid mental model of the full pipeline and be able to reason about why these models behave the way they do in practice, including their characteristic strengths and their failure modes when pushed outside the training distribution.
