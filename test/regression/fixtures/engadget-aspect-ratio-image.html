Artemis II commander shares a remarkable video of Earth vanishing behind the Moon

Reid Wiseman can be forgiven for not just putting his iPhone 17 Pro Max away and enjoying the view.

We've seen some astonishing photos of an Earthset — the Earth setting behind the Moon — from the Artemis II crew's history-making trip around our planet's closest neighbor.

While mission specialist Christina Koch was using a Nikon camera to snap photos, Reid Wiseman, the mission's commander, shared a remarkable video of that same phenomenon.

The short clip offers a mesmerizing perspective of our home planet from the far side of the Moon.
