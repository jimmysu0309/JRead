After an early stutter, the Macintosh created the future.

MEDIUM_BODY_MARK The Apple Macintosh launched on January 24, 1984, but it wasn't until the late 1980s that the machine really found its footing. The original 128K model was under-powered for serious work, and Apple's insistence on closed hardware made it an expensive curiosity rather than a daily driver for most users.
