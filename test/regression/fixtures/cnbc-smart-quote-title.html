Nvidia’s new PC chips represent CEO Huang’s bid to win at every layer of AI stack

Kif Leswing Jordan Novet
NVDA MSFT AMD

As important as Nvidia has become to the tech industry, its entire run-up in recent years has been tied to the data center economics and Wall Street recognizing the threat it poses to incumbents in the space.

During a keynote address at a major computing conference on Monday, Nvidia's CEO said his company plans to build system-on-chips that will reinvent the personal computer for the AI age across the whole stack.
