Remote work — not AI — has sidelined recent college graduates

June 1, 20261:59 PM ET
Heard on Morning Edition

Andrea Hsu

The buzz on college campuses is that AI is disrupting the job market for young college graduates, but new research finds the culprit may be something else entirely: remote work arrangements.

An analysis of federal employment data reveals that companies are less likely to hire recent college grads into occupations that can be done remotely.
