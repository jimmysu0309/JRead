PixyCAD

MAIN_MARK PixyCAD is a new 3D CAD app designed specifically for 3D printing that aims to strike a balance between power and simplicity. The app runs entirely on device with Metal integration, which means you can rotate, pan, and edit models without any lag or need for cloud services.
