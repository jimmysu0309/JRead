Anthropic says evil portrayals of AI were responsible

Fictional portrayals of artificial intelligence can have a real effect on AI models, according to Anthropic, the company said this week in a detailed blog post about its research.

Last year, the company said that during pre-release tests involving a fictional company, an earlier model would often try to blackmail engineers to avoid being replaced by another.

Apparently the company has done more work around that behavior, claiming the original source of the behavior was internet text that portrays AI as evil and interested in self-preservation.

The company went into more detail in a blog post, stating that since its latest model, those models never engage in blackmail during testing where previous models would sometimes do so.
