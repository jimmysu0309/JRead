Remote work has sidelined younger workers

Transcript

The buzz on college campuses is that AI is disrupting the job market for young college graduates, but new research finds the culprit may be something else entirely: remote work arrangements at large employers.

An analysis of federal employment data reveals that companies are less likely to hire recent college grads into occupations that can be done remotely, the researchers found.
