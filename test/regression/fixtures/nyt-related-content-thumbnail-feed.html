A $2.5 Billion Whodunit: The Hack That Dented the U.K. Economy

Last year, hackers burrowed into the computer systems of Jaguar Land Rover and set off a chain of events that would ripple across the British economy for months, according to people familiar with the investigation into the breach and its aftermath.

The attackers warned Jaguar not to seek the help of British authorities and said it would be in touch in 72 hours. The company ignored the warning and invited British investigators and others into its war room in the Midlands to help.
