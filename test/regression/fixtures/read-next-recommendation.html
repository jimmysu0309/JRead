Satellite reveals scale of GPS signal tampering

Deliberate jamming and spoofing of satellite navigation signals have become almost a global emergency over the past five years, affecting tens of thousands of flights.

In the hardest-hit areas, the strength of the GPS signals at the satellite's altitude dropped from the regular 40 decibels to as little as 10 decibels, the team found.
