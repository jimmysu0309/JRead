How LLMs Actually Work

Monday, June 01, 2026 · 26 min
Machine Learning Transformers LLM Neural Networks AI

This article walks through how large language models actually work, from the raw text you type all the way to the tokens they generate in response. Modern LLMs are built by stacking Transformer blocks, so once you understand a single block you have understood the whole machine.

We will cover tokenization, embeddings, the attention mechanism, the feed-forward network, and the residual stream that carries information forward through the layers. Along the way we link to the original Attention Is All You Need paper for the curious reader.

By the end you should have a solid mental model of the full pipeline and be able to reason about why these models behave the way they do in practice, including their strengths and their characteristic failure modes.
