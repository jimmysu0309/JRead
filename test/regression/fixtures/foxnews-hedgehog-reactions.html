By CJ Womack · Fox News

In the wake of democratic socialist Darializa Avila Chevalier defeating New York Rep. Adriano Espaillat in the 13th Congressional District primary, Josh Shapiro is calling on Democrats to reckon with the direction of the party and what it stands for going forward.

Shapiro warned that the party faces a battle over what it believes, urging colleagues to focus on delivering results rather than litigating ideological purity tests that alienate the broader electorate they need to win.
