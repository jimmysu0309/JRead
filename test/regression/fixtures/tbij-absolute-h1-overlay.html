The devout Muslim making a living from Islamophobic AI slop

The week after the outbreak of the US-Iran war, an AI-generated video of Keir Starmer did the rounds on social media, racking up around 400,000 views on Facebook and Instagram. The video shows a convincing likeness of the prime minister at a Downing Street lectern in Islamic dress, claiming the UK is still a proud Muslim country with crazy violent Muslim immigrants being the bedrock of society.

AI-generated videos spreading misinformation and Islamophobia have already become commonplace, especially in the wake of major news events.
