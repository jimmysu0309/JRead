DWARKESH_MAINTITLE_MARK Jensen Huang – TPU competition, why we should sell chips to China, & Nvidia's supply chain moat

"If our next several years are a trillion dollars in scale, we have the supply chain to do it"

DWARKESH_AUTHOR_MARK Dwarkesh Patel · Apr 15, 2026

DWARKESH_INTRO_MARK I asked Jensen about TPU competition, Nvidia's lock on the ever more bottlenecked supply chain needed to make advanced chips, whether we should be selling AI chips to China, why Nvidia doesn't just become a hyperscaler, how it makes its investments, and much more. Enjoy!

DWARKESH_BODY_MARK The rest of the conversation covers supply chain economics, China export controls, and what it means to run inference at trillion-dollar scale. Jensen's view is that scale is destiny: if your product can soak up that much capex, then the supply chain will materialize — because enough money will arrive to force it to. He's betting that Nvidia's job, on the margin, is to accelerate that forcing function.

We then talked about hyperscalers. The conventional wisdom is that Nvidia should vertically integrate — buy or build its own cloud. Jensen disagreed. His framing: every time you add a business, you add a set of decisions that has to be perfect, because the margin structure will punish you otherwise.

Finally, we closed on China. Jensen's position is that the US should sell AI chips to China (with appropriate controls) because the alternative is worse — a Chinese AI ecosystem that is 100% built on Chinese silicon, Chinese compilers, Chinese frameworks, and Chinese research norms.
