No Substitute for Victory

America's Competition With China Must Be Won, Not Managed

MAINTEXT_MARK Amid a presidency beset by failures of deterrence, in Afghanistan, Ukraine, and the Middle East, the administration has strengthened alliances in Asia, restricted adversary access to critical technologies, and endorsed the bipartisan mood for competition. Yet the administration is squandering these early gains by falling into a familiar trap, prioritizing a short term thaw at the expense of a long term victory over a malevolent strategy, with emphasis on process over outcome, and the stakes could not be higher for the alliance system, the regional balance, and the global order that depends on credible deterrence.
