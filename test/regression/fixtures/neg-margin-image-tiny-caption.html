Logitech's new Mobi Fold squeezes a lot of functionality into a tiny folding mouse

Logitech finally announced its new ultraportable travel mouse following leaked marketing images that spoiled the surprise last month. As the name implies, the Mobi Fold is a compact mouse that can fold in half using a hinge that can pivot about 150 degrees.

The arch-shaped Mobi Fold looks similar to other folding devices, like the Surface Arc mouse. But while Microsoft's design folds flat, the Mobi Fold is more like a clamshell cellphone.

For $80, the Mobi Fold delivers a lot of functionality in a package that's easy to slip into a bag or pocket when you're traveling light.
