My Uncle, the Hit Man

Harold Konigsberg was believed to have been responsible for upward of twenty homicides. He was my first true sociopath, and brilliant, brilliant, brilliant, one of his lawyers says. But he could be just adorable.

No one in my family wanted to talk about Harold's life as a contract killer for the Mob. Then one day he called me, and the long silence broke open into a story I had spent my whole life avoiding.

The story of my great-uncle is the story of a man who turned violence into a profession, and of a family that learned to look the other way for as long as it possibly could.
