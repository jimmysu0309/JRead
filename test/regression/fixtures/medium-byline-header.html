The Computer Company That Was Right About Everything Except the Future

DEC built serious machines for serious people then computing stopped being serious

huizhou92
11 min read·Jun 3, 2026

In January 1998, a personal computer company announced it would buy the company that had spent forty years explaining to the world what a serious computer was supposed to be, and the irony of that acquisition still echoes through the history of the industry to this very day.

Digital Equipment Corporation built machines that engineers revered, minicomputers that powered laboratories and universities, and a culture that prized engineering excellence above marketing, a philosophy that would ultimately prove both its greatest strength and its fatal weakness.

The story of DEC is the story of a company that was right about almost everything except the one thing that mattered most, which was where computing was actually heading, and how ordinary people would come to use it.
