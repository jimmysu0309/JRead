David Hockney's 5 Most Famous Paintings

David Hockney has become an icon of British art over his illustrious career, combining a range of different styles to produce his bright, colourful paintings and sculptures. The Yorkshire born artist became an emblem of British Pop Art in the 1960s before moving to California in 1964.

In this article, we take a look at five of Hockney's most famous paintings to trace the development of the artist's style, subject matter and process, from his beginnings in the bohemian London of the 1960s to his colourful depictions of California to his more sombre portraits.

The Splash (1967)

After enjoying newfound success in London, where he had just sold his piece A Rake's Progress (1963) for a large sum, Hockney moved his studio from dreary post-war London to glamorous California, where the sun-drenched atmosphere transformed both his style and subject matter completely.
