Man dead after opening fire at security checkpoint near White House, Secret Service says

Sources identified the suspected gunman as Nasire Best, who had previous encounters with the Secret Service.

A man opened fire at a security checkpoint near the White House Friday evening and was fatally shot by Secret Service agents, according to multiple law enforcement sources. The incident occurred at approximately 8:30 p.m. ET near the South Lawn security perimeter.

Sources identified the suspected gunman as Nasire Best, who had previous encounters with the Secret Service. A bystander was wounded in the crossfire and transported to a local hospital in stable condition, officials said.

The shooting prompted a brief lockdown of the White House complex. President Biden was not at the White House at the time of the incident, having departed earlier Friday for Camp David. Vice President Harris was also not on the premises.

The Secret Service said in a statement that its agents responded to the threat in accordance with standard protective protocols. The agency said an investigation is underway in coordination with the Metropolitan Police Department and the FBI.
