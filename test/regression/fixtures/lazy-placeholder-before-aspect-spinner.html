EXPLAINED: Formula E's new sporting format for the 2026/27 season

Here's an inside look at how Formula E is rewriting the racing rulebook for Season 13.

The ABB FIA Formula E World Championship is introducing a brand-new sporting format for Season 13, designed to deliver more wheel-to-wheel racing and clearer strategy for fans.

As part of this new chapter for the all-electric series, races will now be defined as either a thirty-minute sprint or a forty-five-minute E-Prix, each with its own distinct rules and energy management challenge.

Free Practice has been extended to forty-five minutes, giving teams and drivers more time to dial in their setups before qualifying and the race itself begins in earnest.
