Quantum 101: The State of Quantum Computing

Quantum computing has moved from a theoretical curiosity into a field that governments and corporations are pouring billions of dollars into, and the pace of recent progress has surprised even longtime skeptics who once doubted the machines would ever be practical.

To understand where the field stands today, it helps to separate three distinct threads: sensing, communication, and computation, each of which is maturing at a different rate and carries its own set of engineering challenges and commercial implications for the decade ahead.

Quantum Sensing

Quantum sensors exploit the extreme fragility of quantum states to detect tiny changes in magnetic fields, gravity, and time, and they are already finding real-world applications in navigation, mineral exploration, and medical imaging far beyond the laboratory.
