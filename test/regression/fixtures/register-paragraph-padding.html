Server downtime hits three continents as cloud provider fumbles update

A major cloud service provider caused widespread outages across Europe, Asia and North America on Tuesday after deploying a misconfigured update to its global load balancing infrastructure.

The incident, which lasted approximately four hours, affected thousands of businesses relying on the platform for hosting and content delivery, according to status page updates.

Engineers identified the root cause as an incorrect routing table pushed to edge nodes during a routine maintenance window, the company said in a post-mortem.
